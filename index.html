What is the National Science Bowl?

The National Science Bowl (NSB) is a nationwide academic competition organized by the U.S. Department of Energy. Middle school and high school teams answer questions about science and mathematics in a fast-paced, quiz-style format.

Teams compete in subjects including biology, chemistry, Earth and space science, energy, physics, and mathematics. Each competition team consists of four students, one alternate, and an adult coach. Teams compete at regional tournaments, and regional champions advance to the national finals.

At Fallon Science Bowl Club, students prepare by strengthening their scientific knowledge, practicing questions, and collaborating with teammates.
